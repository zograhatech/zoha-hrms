# Hari HRMS - Product Knowledge Document

## Overview
Hari HRMS (Human Resource Management System) is a comprehensive, modern, web-based platform designed to streamline and automate day-to-day HR operations. Built on the scalable MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) stack, the system offers an intuitive, high-fidelity user experience tailored to both employees and administrative staff.

---

## Unique Selling Points (USPs)
1. **Consumer-Grade Aesthetics in Enterprise Software:** Unlike traditional clunky HR portals, Hari HRMS delivers a beautiful, modern interface featuring dynamic micro-animations, glassmorphism, tailored color palettes, and seamless dark mode support.
2. **Built-in Collaboration:** Eliminates the need for third-party messaging apps with its secure, real-time "Private HRMS Messenger" designed specifically for the organization's workforce.
3. **Engaging Workplace Culture:** Integrates features designed to boost employee morale, such as the "Thursday Fun Chatbot" and automated birthday/anniversary cards, keeping teams engaged beyond just work.
4. **Rich Financial Documents:** Professional, high-fidelity payslips generated with Zoho-like quality directly within the system.

---

## Services & Features We Provide

### 1. Employee Directory & Profiles
- **Centralized Database:** Securely store and search employee records including department, designation, contact info, and role.
- **Rich Profiles:** Employees can update their personal information, profile pictures, and manage settings.

### 2. Time & Attendance Management
- **Visual Attendance Calendar:** Color-coded tracking for Present (Green), Absent (Red), Late (Yellow), Half-Day (Blue), and Holidays (Purple).
- **Punch In/Out:** Track accurate working hours and shifts.

### 3. Leave Management & Approvals
- **Automated Workflow:** Employees can submit leave requests which are routed directly to managers.
- **Status Tracking:** View past and pending leave requests in real time.

### 4. Payroll Administration
- **Automated Calculations:** Automatically calculates earnings, deductions, and final net pay.
- **High-Fidelity Payslips:** View and download professional payslips with detailed breakdowns, company branding, and printable layouts.

### 5. Internal Communications (Live Chat)
- **Private HRMS Messenger:** Real-time messaging tool linking all employees natively inside the application.
- **Rich Media Support:** Allows users to share images, videos, and documents within chats.
- **Group & Direct Messaging:** Supports 1-on-1 chats and team channels.

### 6. Interactive & Engagement Tools
- **Fun Chatbot:** An integrated AI-driven assistant designed to bring fun interactions and help to the workspace. Includes an auto-refresh feature where Jokes, Productive Ideas, and Suggestions are automatically deleted every Sunday to keep content fresh.
- **Toast Notifications:** A system-wide alert mechanism providing beautiful, consistent popup feedback for user actions.

### 7. IT & Support Tickets
- **Internal Helpdesk:** Employees can raise tickets for IT support, HR grievances, or administrative help, tracked centrally for quick resolution.

### 8. System Preferences
- **Theme Support:** Fluid transition between Light and Dark mode using the integrated Theme Context.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized to view flawlessly across various devices with rich, responsive layouts.

### 9. Comprehensive Administrative Command Center
Offering expansive control for Administrators and HR Managers, functioning as the primary command center.
- **Real-Time Analytics Dashboard:** A robust real-time overview displaying total headcounts, new joiners, monthly payroll costs, pending leave requests, open IT tickets, and workforce demographics (by role, by department).
- **Shift Roster & Attendance Controls:** Complete control over organizational shift patterns, time rosters, and visual area-charts tracking 7-day attendance trends.
- **Onboarding & Recruitment:** Dedicated pipelines to track the recruitment workflow and oversee new joiner integrations.
- **Performance & Exit Management:** Capable of tracking employee grades and coordinating the entire resignation workflow (last working day scheduling, final settlements).
- **Asset Management:** Centralized indexing to log, assign, and track company hardware (computers, accessories) distributed to the workforce.
- **Zoom Integration:** Syncs native Zoom meetings intelligently placing upcoming meetings right inside the central HR dashboard calendars.

### 10. Integrated Tally & ERP Infrastructure
Hari HRMS uniquely embeds heavy-duty financial and ERP management directly into the workforce platform, replacing the need for external tools.
- **Core Tally Accounting:** A native Double-Entry bookkeeping system featuring Ledger group mapping (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, Expenses), Voucher logging (Journal, Contra, Payment, Receipt), and real-time computation of Company Net Worth.
- **Dynamic Balance Sheets:** Live-generated balance sheets reflecting categorized assets and liabilities, fully exportable to CSV identically to traditional Tally setups.
- **Inventory & Stock Management:** Comprehensive material tracking and internal stock flow oversight.
- **Sales & Purchases:** Full workflows for managing client invoicing, processing accounts receivable, triggering procurement flows, and accounts payable logging seamlessly tied into the ledgers.
- **Banking & Taxation:** Features dedicated controls for banking reconciliations and automated GST/Taxation bracket management out-of-the-box.

### 11. Learning Management System (LMS)
A complete educational ecosystem designed to foster professional growth and skill development.
- **Course Administration:** HR can create categorized courses with multiple lessons.
- **Multimedia Content:** Supports video lessons, reading materials, and resource downloads.
- **Interactive Quizzes:** Built-in quiz engine for employee knowledge verification and certification.
- **Progress Tracking:** Real-time visibility into enrollment rates and completion statuses for individual employees.

### 12. Global Document Management
A unified storage layer for critical employee and organizational documents.
- **Digital Vault:** Securely upload and retrieve personal ID proofs, certificates, and employment contracts.
- **Exit Document Management:** Automated collection and verification of documents during the offboarding process (Relieving Letter, Experience Certificates).
- **Role-Based Access:** Granular permissions ensuring document visibility only to authorized HR staff and the individual owner.

### 13. External Verification & Security
- **Credential Verification Portal:** A public-facing gateway for external organizations to verify the employment history and certificates of ex-employees (with consent).
- **System-Wide Audit Logs:** Detailed records of critical actions (logins, payroll changes, document deletions) for compliance and accountability.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Readiness:** Ready to integrate secure mobile verification for administrative actions.
