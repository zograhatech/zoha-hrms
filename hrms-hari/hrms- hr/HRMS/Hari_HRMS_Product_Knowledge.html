Hari HRMS - Product Knowledge Document

Overview

Hari HRMS (Human Resource Management System) is a comprehensive, modern, web-based platform designed to streamline and automate day-to-day HR operations. Built on the scalable MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) stack, the system offers an intuitive, high-fidelity user experience tailored to both employees and administrative staff.

Unique Selling Points (USPs)

  • Consumer-Grade Aesthetics: Modern interface with dynamic micro-animations, glassmorphism, and dark mode support.
  • Built-in Collaboration: Secure, real-time "Private HRMS Messenger" native to the platform.
  • Engaging Workplace Culture: Thursday Fun Chatbot, automated cards, and morale-boosting modules.
  • Rich Financial Documents: Professional, high-fidelity payslips generated with premium quality.

Services & Features We Provide

1. Employee Directory & Profiles

  • Centralized Database: Securely store and search employee records including department and role.
  • Rich Profiles: Personal information management, profile pictures, and notification settings.

2. Time & Attendance Management

  • Visual Attendance Calendar: Color-coded tracking for Present, Absent, Late, and Holidays.
  • Punch In/Out: Accurate daily tracking for working hours and shifts.

3. Leave Management & Approvals

  • Automated Workflow: Direct manager-routing for faster approval cycles.
  • Status Tracking: Real-time visibility for pending and past leave history.

4. Payroll Administration

  • Automated Calculations: Computes earnings, statutory deductions, and net pay.
  • High-Fidelity Payslips: Customizable, printable layouts with complete tax breakdowns.

5. Internal Communications (Live Chat)

  • Private HRMS Messenger: Native real-time messaging with group and direct channels.
  • Rich Media Support: Seamless sharing of images, videos, and documents within conversations.

6. Interactive & Engagement Tools

  • Fun Chatbot: AI-driven engagement assistant with automated weekly content refreshes.
  • Toast Notifications: Consistent, beautiful popup feedback across the system.

7. IT & Support Tickets

  • Internal Helpdesk: Centralized ticketing for IT support, HR grievances, or administrative requests.

8. System Preferences

  • Theme Support: Seamless Light/Dark mode transitions.
  • Responsive Design: Optimized layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile views.

9. Comprehensive Administrative Command Center

  • Real-Time Analytics: Dashboard for headcounts, payroll costs, and workforce demographics.
  • Shift Roster Controls: Management of organizational shift patterns and trends.
  • Onboarding & Recruitment: Visual pipelines for hiring and integration.
  • Asset Management: Inventory indexing for hardware assignment.
  • Zoom Integration: Native meeting scheduling synced with dashboards.

10. Integrated Tally & ERP Infrastructure

  • Core Tally Accounting: Full double-entry bookkeeping, Ledgers, and Vouchers.
  • Dynamic Balance Sheets: Live-generated, exportable financial statements.
  • Inventory & Stock: Real-time material tracking and procurement flows.
  • Banking & Taxation: GST/Tax management and banking reconciliation out-of-the-box.

11. Learning Management System (LMS)

  • Course Admin: Creation of categorized digital curriculums.
  • Multimedia Content: Support for video lessons and resource downloads.
  • Interactive Quizzes: Native engine for knowledge verification and certifications.

12. Global Document Management

  • Digital Vault: Secure storage for personal IDs, contracts, and certificates.
  • Exit Documents: Automated relieving papers and experience certificate generation.
  • Access Control: Strict role-based visibility to ensure data privacy.

13. External Verification & Security

  • Verification Portal: Public-facing gateway for external history checks of ex-employees.
  • System Audit Logs: Transparent tracking of all sensitive administrative actions.
